How the implied value is built, and the evidence behind every category multiple. A multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the category's live-token comparables, shrunk toward its family of related categories and then the whole live market. Categories with no comparable of their own take their family's median; a family with no comparable falls back to the global median. One comparable's ratio is clipped at 5.0 before it is used: a token worth hundreds of times the value locked is a tiny-TVL outlier, not a category's typical outcome. No multiple exceeds 0.75.

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chain spread
